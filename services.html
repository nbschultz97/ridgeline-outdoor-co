Services

Adopt, foster, or volunteer.

Three ways to help. Pick one, or do all three — every option keeps an animal fed, safe, and closer to a permanent home.

Adoption process

Browse available animals below or in person at our Saint Paul adoption center. Every adoption includes a vet exam, up-to-date vaccinations, and spay or neuter surgery already completed. Fees range from $75 for small animals to $250 for dogs, and cover only a fraction of actual veterinary cost. Meet-and-greets are free and require no appointment during open hours.

Foster program

Fostering is a temporary home, not a lifetime commitment — most placements run two to six weeks while an animal recovers, grows old enough for surgery, or simply needs a break from the shelter environment. We supply food, a crate or carrier, litter, and 24/7 phone support from our vet team. No fostering experience is required, and we match animals to your household, schedule, and any pets you already have.

Volunteer roles

Dog walkers and cat socializers commit to a weekly two-hour shift. Transport volunteers drive animals between partner shelters and vet appointments, usually once or twice a month. Event volunteers help staff adoption fairs on weekends. Every role starts with a 30-minute orientation, no prior animal-care experience needed.
