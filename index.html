Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a nonprofit foster-based shelter. We rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome dogs, cats, and small animals across the metro — and we can’t do it without volunteers, fosters, and adopters like you.

How you can help.

  • ♥

    Adopt

    Every adoption includes a vet exam, vaccinations, and spay or neuter. Meet-and-greets are always free, no appointment required.

  • ⌂

    Foster

    We provide food, supplies, and 24/7 vet support. You provide a couch and a little patience. Most fosters last two to six weeks.

  • ✦

    Volunteer

    Dog walking, cat socialization, transport, and event support. No experience needed — just a few hours a month.
