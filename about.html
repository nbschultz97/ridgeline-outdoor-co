About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Foster-based, since 2009.

A volunteer-run nonprofit that believes every animal, regardless of age, breed, or medical history, deserves a real chance at a home.

Our story

Twin Cities Animal Rescue started in 2009 in a founder’s garage in Saint Paul with three foster dogs and a waiting list of one. We’ve never operated a large kennel facility — every animal in our care lives in a volunteer foster home until they’re adopted, which keeps stress low and lets us learn an animal’s real personality before matching them with a family. Today we work with over 120 active foster families across the metro.

What we won’t do

We won’t euthanize a healthy or treatable animal to make space — we’ll find another foster home instead. We won’t adopt out an animal without a vet exam, vaccinations, and spay or neuter completed first. And we won’t turn away a volunteer because they lack prior animal-care experience; every role starts with training.

Our partners

We work closely with Ramsey County Animal Control, the Minnesota Spay Neuter Assistance Program, and three regional transport networks that bring animals to us from overcrowded shelters outside the metro. Local businesses including Pawsitive Vet Clinic and Twin Cities Pet Supply donate veterinary care and food throughout the year.

A message from our shelter director.

“Every animal that comes through our door has a story we don’t always know. What we do know is that a foster home, a patient volunteer, or a family willing to adopt an older or shy animal can change that story completely. In seventeen years we’ve placed over 2,400 animals, and every single one started with someone deciding to help. That someone could be you.”
