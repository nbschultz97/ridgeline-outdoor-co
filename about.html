About Ridgeline

Built in Truckee. Repaired forever.

A small shop in the Sierra making backcountry gear we use ourselves — and standing behind it for life.

How we started

Ridgeline began in 2014 when our founder, Mae Chen, sewed her first pack in a Truckee garage after a major-brand pack failed mid-trip on the John Muir Trail. She sold six packs the first year. By year three, we were a four-person shop. Today we are still four people, plus our repair tech Luis, plus Mae.

Our repair promise

Send any Ridgeline product back at any point in its life. We'll repair it for $20 plus parts. We've repaired packs from 2014, 2016, and 2019 in the last twelve months. The original 2014 packs are still in service — and now they're more comfortable than they were new.

What we won't do

We won't release a product without at least 90 nights of field testing. We won't put a PFC DWR on anything. We won't sell to resellers at margins that force our customers to overpay. These rules have cost us short-term growth. They've also kept us in business for twelve years.
